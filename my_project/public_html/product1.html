What Are Digital Wallets?

Digital wallets store your payment information securely and allow you to make transactions using your smartphone or computer.

What is UPI?

UPI (Unified Payments Interface) is a real-time payment system developed by NPCI that allows fund transfer between two bank accounts on a mobile platform.

Safety Tips for Using Digital Payments

  • Use official apps from trusted providers (e.g., Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm).
  • Enable two-factor authentication for added security.
  • Never share your UPI PIN or OTP with anyone.
  • Regularly review your transaction history.
  • Set transaction limits to prevent large unauthorized transfers.
