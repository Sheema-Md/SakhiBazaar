Cybersecurity Tips

Protect your devices and accounts from fraud and cyber threats.

  • Use Strong, Unique Passwords: Create passwords with letters, numbers, and symbols. Don’t reuse passwords.
  • Enable Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA): Adds an extra layer of protection.
  • Keep Software Updated: Install updates to fix security vulnerabilities.
  • Be Wary of Phishing Scams: Don’t click on suspicious links or open unknown attachments.
  • Use Secure Wi-Fi: Avoid public Wi-Fi for sensitive tasks or use a VPN.
  • Backup Your Data: Use external drives or cloud storage to regularly save important files.
  • Limit Personal Info Online: Be cautious about what you share on social media.
  • Install Antivirus Software: Use reputable antivirus and keep it up to date.
  • Log Out After Use: Especially on shared or public devices.
  • Monitor Your Accounts: Watch for unusual activity and set up alerts.
